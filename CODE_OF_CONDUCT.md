# Public Participation Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The M5 AI Sovereign Commons invites rigorous, good-faith review from people with
different legal, technical, professional, geographic, and lived experience.
Disagreement is expected. Harassment, intimidation, disclosure of protected
information, and manipulation of the review process are not.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- address claims, evidence, specifications, examples, and tests rather than
  attacking people;
- identify uncertainty and distinguish fact, interpretation, proposal, and
  personal experience;
- cite authoritative sources where a claim depends on law, regulation,
  standards, provider behavior, or current external facts;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, accessibility, and cultural differences;
- disclose material conflicts of interest when relevant to a contribution;
- correct material mistakes through an attributable update;
- follow the repository's security-reporting and nonpublic-information rules.

## Prohibited conduct

Participants must not:

- harass, threaten, demean, stalk, or discriminate against another participant;
- publish personal data, credentials, private correspondence, confidential
  records, privileged material, or nonpublic pilot information;
- post exploitable vulnerability details through a public channel;
- impersonate another person or organization or misrepresent authority,
  credentials, endorsement, partnership, certification, or regulatory status;
- coordinate spam, deceptive engagement, vote manipulation, or disruption;
- encourage speculation intended to identify a nonpublic pilot target or
  counterparty;
- use a public contribution to solicit an investment, announce an offering, or
  imply legal or governmental approval.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content; limit participation; or
refer a matter to the appropriate accountable authority when necessary to
protect participants or the integrity of the review.

Good-faith criticism of M5, its proposals, its maintainers, or its governance is
not a violation. Enforcement decisions should identify the conduct at issue,
apply the narrowest reasonable response, and preserve an appeal or correction
path where appropriate.

## Reporting

Report participation concerns to
[support@titlechainfoundation.org](mailto:support@titlechainfoundation.org).
Report vulnerabilities only through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Do not include sensitive evidence in an initial message. Maintainers will
provide an approved transfer path when necessary.
