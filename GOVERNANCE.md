# Governance

## Purpose

The M5 AI Sovereign Commons maintains vendor-neutral public specifications for
bounded, accountable use of AI and automated capabilities.

## Decision states

1. **Proposed** — submitted for initial discussion.
2. **Draft** — accepted for structured public review.
3. **Candidate** — implementation and conformance feedback is being evaluated.
4. **Stable** — approved through a separately documented release decision.
5. **Superseded** — retained for version and interoperability history.

A repository label, filename, ZIP name, test pass, or agent statement cannot
advance a specification between states.

## Change requirements

Material normative changes require:

- a public rationale;
- review of authority, privacy, security, and interoperability effects;
- updated tests or examples where applicable;
- a version decision;
- approval through the repository's documented review process.

Provider prices, model identifiers, API behavior, rate limits, and retention
terms are dated configuration facts. They are not M5 canonical truth and must
not silently change normative authority rules.

## Human and institutional authority

No model, agent, wallet, credential, API, employee account, contractor account,
plugin, or external system is an independent principal. The accountable human
or legal entity and the applicable external law remain authoritative.

## Public/private boundary

Public review must not require or expose:

- private M5POD evidence;
- personal credentials or identity records;
- secrets, keys, tokens, or production endpoints;
- confidential M5Canon rules or internal operational material;
- non-public legal, commercial, or security records.

Maintainers may remove such material from public review surfaces and coordinate
appropriate history removal where necessary.
