# M5-CV Activation Bridge to People's Trust

![Public waitlist, controlled activation, capability, Commons, and pilot pathway](../../assets/activation-pathway.svg)

People's Trust is the first public sample-data pilot used to demonstrate how a
broad range of human capabilities can map into a bounded community-project
workflow.

The pathway is:

```text
Learn / Teach / Earn
        |
        v
M5-CV
        |
        v
Tools + Training
        |
        v
Public Commons
        |
        v
People's Trust pilot
```

M5-CV is the person's growing capability record. The Public Commons is the open
learning and review environment. People's Trust supplies a concrete sample-data
workflow requiring many forms of human capability, including accounting, real
estate, lending, research, operations, development, data, notary, and community
stewardship.

## Boundary

The pilot uses sample, non-production records. Joining the waitlist, entering a
controlled activation pathway, building an M5-CV, or reviewing the simulation
does not itself create title, ownership, cooperative membership, voting rights,
investment rights, economic rights, a job, or authority to act for a project.

Any future cooperative, operating entity, membership structure, or economic
participation must have its own adopted legal and governance structure,
eligibility rules, affirmative consent, accountable authority, disclosures,
and recorded admission.

## Why this matters

The Foundation can evaluate whether the pathway helps people build portable,
verifiable capability and whether those capabilities can map into bounded
real-world roles without collapsing identity, title, authority, stewardship,
and economic rights into one system.
