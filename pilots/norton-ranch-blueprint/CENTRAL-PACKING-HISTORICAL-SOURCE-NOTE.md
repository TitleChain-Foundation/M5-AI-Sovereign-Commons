# Historical Source Note — Central Packing Co.

The founder's note states that Central Packing Co. was among the early group of Colorado meat companies exporting American beef to Japan in the 1960s.

This statement is recorded from Norton family/company history supplied by Pamela Norton.

Independent public records also place a `Central Packing Co., Inc.` among meat-industry plaintiffs in a 1966 federal case in the District of Colorado, establishing a contemporaneous public record of a Central Packing Co. operating in the Colorado meat industry during that period.

Additional family, trade, export, customs or company records should be added here as they are digitized so the Japan-export history can be preserved with primary-source evidence.

The repository should use **"among the early group of Colorado companies exporting beef to Japan in the 1960s"** rather than an unsupported singular "first company" claim unless a primary source establishes that distinction.
