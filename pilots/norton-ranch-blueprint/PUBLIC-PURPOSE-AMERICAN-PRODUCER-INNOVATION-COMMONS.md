# Public Purpose — An Innovation Commons for American Producers

## Mission

M5 Sovereign Agriculture is public infrastructure intended to lower the cost of innovation for American farmers, ranchers, growers, producers, cooperatives, family businesses and independent agricultural entrepreneurs.

The Commons should make it possible to begin with open standards, interoperable schemas, properly licensed research data, local-first reference software, device provisioning patterns, farmer-controlled data architecture, provenance, export and portability tools, conformance tests, and reference business patterns for independent hardware and service companies.

The goal is not to create one new agricultural platform. The goal is to make it easier to create **thousands of independent businesses** without each one rebuilding the same identity, registry, provenance, privacy and local-compute foundation.

## Open choice, not exclusion

This Commons is not based on nationality, ancestry or birthplace.

The distinction is between **accountable participation and extractive capture**. A domestic corporation can be extractive. A foreign-founded company can be a good partner. The questions are practical: Does the farmer control the farm's data? Can the farmer export it? Can the farmer change vendors? Can local companies interoperate? Can hardware be repaired or replaced? Is secondary model training disclosed and authorized? Does the system work locally when appropriate? Are recurring charges transparent? Can independent entrepreneurs compete? Does value have a path back to the people and communities producing it?

## Who this is for

This repository should remain usable by a one-family operation as well as a large producer. The reference architecture should not assume that the user has a venture capital budget, a cloud engineering team or thousands of animals. A small operator should be able to begin small and keep ownership of the resulting records as the operation grows.
