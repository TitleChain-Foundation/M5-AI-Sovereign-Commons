`PENDING_EXTERNAL` means the authoritative participant, act, or evidence has not been supplied. `SIMULATED_PASS` means code can demonstrate an internal completeness rule only. It is not a legal, professional, fiduciary, regulatory, or financial approval.

## Stakeholder views

- **Agency/public owner:** unresolved roles stay `TBD`; only official records establish pathway, title, approvals, permits, notices, and public authority.
- **Capital provider/investor:** the tranche buys no public fee title; terms, eligibility, risk, economics, and rights require definitive documents.
- **Bank/escrow/fiduciary:** no model or fixture can issue a valid settlement instruction or release funds.
- **Professionals:** counsel, architect, engineer, environmental specialist, accountant, auditor, and tax advisers retain their defined judgment and certification domains.
- **Workers/community:** wages, co-op rights, governance and public benefits are separately documented; they are not implied securities or deed interests.
- **Developers:** code validates structure and deterministic prerequisites, routes exceptions, and emits receipts. It does not manufacture authority or convert targets into results.
