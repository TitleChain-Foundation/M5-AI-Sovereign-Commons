## Requested action

The applicant requests NPS review of the attached Program of Preservation and Utilization and, if the statutory and program requirements are met, an NPS recommendation that GSA proceed with a Historic Monument Public Benefit Conveyance to the applicant.

No approval is presumed. The official federal record controls all findings, recommendations, terms, and transfer decisions.

## Proposed public-purpose summary

The simulation proposes preservation and phased reuse as a publicly stewarded civic commons supporting compatible civic, cultural, educational, workforce, small-business, occupancy, and resilience functions. Final uses depend on community process, professional diligence, preservation compatibility, public approvals, and demonstrated operating and financial capacity.

## Simulated certifications

A real authorized applicant would certify, in the form required by GSA/NPS, that:

- it is eligible and authorized to apply and accept the property and restrictions;
- application information is accurate and complete to the signer's knowledge;
- it will use and maintain the property for the approved historic/public purpose;
- it has or will secure capacity to repair, rehabilitate, operate, preserve, and maintain the property;
- revenue-producing activity, leases, changes, and accounting will comply with the deed, approved program, statute, and agency requirements;
- required reports, records, inspections, audits, and corrective actions will be supported; and
- material changes will be submitted for required approval.
