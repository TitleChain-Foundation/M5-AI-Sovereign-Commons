# DOC-06 — Capital Escrow and Draw Agreement (summary)

**Status: Draft, for demonstration only.**

A draft framework for how project capital would be held and released through a regulated escrow agent as rehabilitation milestones are met (predevelopment, construction, and related accounts). Explicitly states that no reader should infer the property is currently owned by the project, that federal conveyance has been approved, or that any capital provider is committed — all of that remains subject to signed definitive documents.
