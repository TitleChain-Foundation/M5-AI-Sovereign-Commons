# DOC-04 — Master Lease Term Sheet (summary)

**Status: Draft, for demonstration only.**

Outlines a proposed long-term lease structure between a future qualifying public landowner (TBD) and the 312 Spring Commons operating entity (TBD). The intent is to keep fee title with a public owner while granting the operator long-term rights to rehabilitate, finance, and run the property under federal historic requirements and public-owner approval. No landlord, tenant, rent, or term is yet fixed.
