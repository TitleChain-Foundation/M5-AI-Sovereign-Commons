# DOC-02 — Conditional Capital Commitment Letter (summary)

**Status: Draft, for demonstration only.**

A working form for soft-circled or conditional capital commitments to 312 Spring Commons. It lets a prospective capital provider indicate a conditional intent to fund, subject to final legal, tax, and project-structure review. It is not a binding commitment and creates no obligation to fund. The full form is available on request — see `../request-access.md` if it isn't yet posted in `../public/`.
