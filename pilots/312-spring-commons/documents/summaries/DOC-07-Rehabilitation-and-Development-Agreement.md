# DOC-07 — Rehabilitation and Development Agreement (summary)

**Status: Draft, for demonstration only.**

A draft project-delivery framework covering preservation, design, construction, and phased activation of the building, contingent on a lawful federal conveyance and NPS/public-owner approvals. States plainly that the project is still in diligence and that the GSA's ~$153M capital-maintenance figure is not a verified construction budget — the ~$200M–$240M planning envelope is preliminary only.
