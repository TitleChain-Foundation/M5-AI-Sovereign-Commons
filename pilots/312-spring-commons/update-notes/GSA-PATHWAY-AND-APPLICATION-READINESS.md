## 2. The $25M is not a GSA purchase payment under the working pathway

The project currently assumes a **Historic Monument Public Benefit Conveyance** under 40 U.S.C. § 550(h), not a competitive cash purchase.

Under that statute:

- GSA may convey qualifying surplus historic property to an eligible state, political subdivision or instrumentality, or municipality **without monetary consideration to the United States**;
- the Secretary of the Interior/NPS must determine the property suitable and desirable for historic-monument use;
- revenue-producing activity requires approval of the grantee's repair, rehabilitation, restoration, maintenance, financing, and accounting arrangements; and
- the deed carries perpetual historic-use and potential federal reversion conditions.

Accordingly, the proposed $25M could become part of the applicant's evidence that rehabilitation and early project obligations can be financed. It should not be described as:

- a payment to GSA;
- a bid price;
- a deposit that secures the property;
- consideration that compels an award;
- proof that GSA or NPS approved the project; or
- committed capital unless an authorized provider and project entity execute binding documents and all stated conditions are satisfied.

A safer current statement is:

> The project is seeking evidence-backed capacity for an initial $25 million rehabilitation tranche, contingent on confirmation of the federal disposition pathway, selection and authorization of an eligible public grantee, approval of the preservation/use/repair/financial plans, definitive financing documents, and all required public, legal, fiduciary, and regulated-provider approvals. No GSA purchase payment, bid deposit, or committed financing is currently represented.

## 4. Required first submission: expression of interest and pathway inquiry

The official GSA historic-conveyance business process begins when a state government submits an expression of interest in a surplus federally owned building to the appropriate federal point of contact. For Spring Commons, the first controlled submission should be an **eligible-public-applicant Expression of Interest and Request for Pathway Confirmation**, not an investor term sheet.

### Minimum contents

1. **Applicant identity and eligibility**
   - exact legal name and governmental form;
   - statutory/charter authority relevant to acquiring and holding the property;
   - governing-body resolution or other authority to make the inquiry;
   - named accountable official and authorized contact.
2. **Property identification**
   - official property name and address;
   - GSA property/disposal identifier, if assigned;
   - explicit request for the authoritative title/legal-description/property package.
3. **Requested pathway**
   - request to confirm current federal-use, excess, surplus, screening, and disposal state;
   - expression of interest in a Historic Monument Public Benefit Conveyance under 40 U.S.C. § 550(h), subject to eligibility and agency review;
   - request for the responsible GSA disposal officer and NPS regional contact.
4. **Preliminary public purpose**
   - concise historic-monument/public-benefit use concept;
   - explicit preservation and public-access intent;
   - clear separation between public fee title and any proposed operator or lessee.
5. **Capacity statement**
   - planning-stage rehabilitation and operations concept;
   - current sources of predevelopment capacity;
   - accurate status of the proposed $25M tranche—illustrative, soft-circled, conditionally committed, or binding—and supporting evidence for whichever status is claimed.
6. **Requests to GSA/NPS**
   - property-specific eligibility and process confirmation;
   - current notice, instructions, deadlines, and evaluation requirements;
   - available due-diligence materials and site-access process;
   - required Section 106, preservation-clause, deferred-maintenance, environmental, and deed work; and
   - confirmation whether any competitive sale, negotiated sale, or other authority controls instead.

### Required signatory

The expression should come from the eligible public applicant or an official demonstrably authorized to act for it. TitleChain Foundation, the proposed Public Trust, an investor, or a project developer should not imply that it can substitute for the eligible governmental applicant.

## 5. Formal NPS application package

The NPS Historic Surplus Property Program states that a formal application must be developed by the eligible applicant and submitted to the appropriate NPS regional office. Its primary component is the **Program of Preservation and Utilization**.

The official NPS brochure identifies three major sections:

1. **Preservation plan**
2. **Use plan**
3. **Financial plan**

The GSA historic-conveyance business process additionally identifies a **repair plan** and **proposed rehabilitative changes** in the formal application and review flow. The property-specific request from GSA/NPS controls if its requirements differ or are more detailed.

## 6. What “$25M secured” would require

Use staged labels and do not skip states:

| Status | Minimum evidence | Permitted statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ILLUSTRATIVE` | Planning model only | “Illustrative $25M tranche” |
| `INVESTOR-IDENTIFIED` | Verified provider identity and accountable contact | “Potential provider identified” |
| `INDICATION-OF-INTEREST` | Signed non-binding indication with assumptions | “Non-binding interest received” |
| `CONDITIONALLY-COMMITTED` | Executed binding commitment by authorized parties, defined instrument/economics, conditions, expiry, remedies, and counsel review | “$25M conditionally committed, subject to listed conditions” |
| `FUNDS-VERIFIED` | Independent proof of funds/capacity and completed required diligence | “Funding capacity verified as of [date] by [qualified verifier]” |
| `ESCROW-RESERVED` | Executed escrow agreement, deposited/reserved funds, verified bank record, release conditions | “$25M reserved in escrow subject to stated release conditions” |
| `AVAILABLE-FOR-APPROVED-USE` | All applicable federal/public, legal, fiduciary, instrument, and draw conditions satisfied | “Funds available for the authorized purpose” |

The present package is at **`ILLUSTRATIVE`**. DOC-02 can support a later state, but only after the blanks, parties, authority, binding terms, conditions, and evidence are completed. The synthetic receipt must never be used as proof of funds.

## 8. Immediate action sequence

1. Identify a willing, plausibly eligible state/local governmental applicant.
2. Obtain its counsel's preliminary eligibility and authority analysis.
3. Ask GSA in writing for the property's current status, controlling disposition pathway, notice, deadlines, responsible disposal officer, and property package.
4. If GSA confirms Historic Monument PBC screening, submit the applicant-authorized expression of interest.
5. Obtain the current NPS regional application instructions and organize the Program of Preservation and Utilization.
6. Commission site, preservation, repair, use, cost, operating, and financial work needed for the application.
7. Convert any $25M capital interest into accurately classified evidence; do not call it secured until the corresponding evidence state is met.
8. If GSA instead confirms a public sale, stop the PBC workflow and build a separate property-specific bid package from the official Invitation for Bids.
