## Core concept boundary

The Public Bank of California is a **research and simulation concept**. It may
be used to test one possible public-capital/wholesale participation layer around
productive California assets.

It is not represented as:

- a chartered bank;
- a California state agency;
- an FDIC-insured depository institution;
- an operating lender;
- an approved source of Spring Commons capital;
- a registered transfer agent, broker-dealer, exchange, ATS, custodian or
  clearing agency; or
- an offer of banking, deposit, lending or investment services.

## SEC implementation extension

The existing SEC implementation crosswalk should be extended with the following
test boundary:

```text
PUBLIC / REAL-ASSET RECORD
federal disposition → public title → deed/use restrictions
        ↓
PROJECT / OPERATING RECORD
public owner → lease/operator → permits → project obligations
        ↓
MONEY RECORD
regulated bank / escrow / payment rail
        ↓
INVESTMENT / ECONOMIC-RIGHT RECORD
issuer / instrument / investor / restrictions
        ↓
SECURITIES RECORD IF APPLICABLE
responsible registered transfer agent / official holder record
        ↓
TITLECHAIN / M5 EVIDENCE
authority + instruction + approval + execution + reconciliation
```

No lower layer silently becomes the authoritative record of the layer above it.

## Public Bank concept controls to add to the Spring review set

1. **Charter/authority gate** — no bank capability unless an actual lawful
   institution and its authority are established.
2. **Deposit boundary** — the concept may not be described as receiving deposits.
3. **Money-record boundary** — bank/escrow records remain authoritative for
   actual money movement.
4. **Securities boundary** — transfer-agent/securityholder records remain
   authoritative where applicable.
5. **Public-funds authority** — appropriations, grants, deposits or public
   commitments require their actual lawful source and approvals.
6. **Community-institution interoperability** — model participation with
   community banks and credit unions without presuming replacement or control.
7. **Capital-provenance path** — source, vehicle, remitting institution,
   settlement rail, destination and instrument remain separately evidenced.
8. **No technical-authority substitution** — M5 accounts, namespaces, wallets,
   code, TitleChain receipts and AI do not create a banking charter or public
   authority.

## Cross-document implications

### README

Add the Public Bank concept as a separate concept section after the SEC
implementation discussion and before detailed capitalization. Link the essay and
concept visual.

### SEC implementation crosswalk

Add a Public Bank concept subsection explaining the bank/escrow/transfer-agent
separation and the eight controls above.

### DOC-01 / Master Deal Term Sheet

If a public-capital institution is modeled, list it only as **TBD / concept /
subject to legal authority and final financing structure**. Do not insert it as
a committed lender.

### DOC-02 / Conditional Capital Commitment

Do not identify the Public Bank concept as a committed capital provider. Any
future public-capital participant must have legal identity, authority, source of
funds, signatory authority and applicable regulated status independently
verified.

### DOC-06 / Capital Escrow + Draw

Continue to treat the selected regulated bank/escrow record as the authoritative
money-movement record. Public-bank concept data is planning/reference data only
until a lawful institution actually participates.

### DOC-10 / TitleChain Evidence + Digital Records

Add a record-class distinction among:

- `PUBLIC_CAPITAL_CONCEPT`;
- `AUTHORIZED_PUBLIC_FINANCE_ENTITY`;
- `REGULATED_BANK_OR_CREDIT_UNION`;
- `ESCROW_OR_PAYMENT_RECORD`;
- `SECURITIES_TRANSFER_AGENT_RECORD`; and
- `TITLECHAIN_EVIDENCE_RECORD`.

These states may link but must never be inferred from one another.

### DOC-11 / Private Offering / PPM

Do not describe a public-bank concept as placement, custody, transfer,
underwriting or market infrastructure.

### DOC-15 / Risk Factors

Add risks for:

- public misunderstanding of a concept as a chartered bank or state program;
- assumed public funding without appropriation/authority;
- bank/transfer-agent/title record conflation;
- regulatory classification error;
- public-private conflict or procurement assumptions;
- unsupported credit enhancement or government-backing assumptions.

### DOC-17 / Authority + Jurisdiction + Capital Provenance Matrix

If the Public Bank concept appears, mark:

- legal entity: `NOT ESTABLISHED`;
- charter/status: `NOT ESTABLISHED`;
- government authority: `NONE`;
- project commitment: `NONE`;
- transfer-agent status: `NONE`;
- bank/escrow system of record: `N/A UNTIL LAWFUL PARTICIPANT SELECTED`.

### DOC-21 / Benchmark Methodology

Potential concept-test metrics may include:

- percentage of modeled public-capital events with explicit authority source;
- percentage correctly routed to a real bank/escrow or transfer-agent system of
  record;
- number of blocked actions caused by missing charter/authority evidence; and
- reconciliation mismatch rate across project/title/money/security records.

### DOC-23 / Capital Provenance

Add explicit public-capital source type and authority-source fields without
treating a state or municipal label as proof that funds are public, appropriated
or authorized.
