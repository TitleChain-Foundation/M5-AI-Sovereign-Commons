### v0.6 tightening — open entity grounding + source-chain activation

v0.6 keeps the 23-document package and strengthens the evidence model rather than adding another document. In particular:

- **OpenData.org** is added as an optional open entity-reference layer for public reproducibility; reported coverage counts are dated external-source metadata, not conformance requirements.
- **Overture Maps / GERS** is added as an optional spatial/place grounding layer; location identity remains separate from legal-entity identity and legal authority.
- Each participant may maintain a **canonical identifier crosswalk** linking M5/TitleChain IDs to official registration numbers and, where available, LEI/CIK, BrightQuery, OpenCorporates, OpenData.org, Overture/GERS, JNR and technical alias references.
- `SOURCE_CHAIN_ACTIVATED` becomes an explicit evidence gate only after required entity, accountable-authority, source-of-funds, jurisdiction-path, conflict/side-arrangement and regulatory-routing evidence is sufficiently current.
- `DESTINATION_CHAIN_CONFIRMED` is a separate state based on destination-side authoritative evidence.
- Historical jurisdiction paths, corrections and supersessions are versioned rather than overwritten.
- M5Global analytics distinguish **OBSERVED / ESTIMATED / MODELED / SCENARIO / FORECAST** outputs and apply aggregation, delay, banding or suppression when small cohorts could create re-identification or misleading market signals.
- Open-reference data use must preserve dataset/provider, license/attribution, release/snapshot, access date, transformations, match method/confidence, known gaps and correction history.


## Canonical v0.6 rules

1. **No material capital or economic/control right should operate outside the disclosed authority/evidence graph.**
2. **A lawful SPV/fund/trust/holding company is a node, not an opacity wall.**
3. **Material side arrangements may be confidential but may not silently change economics, control, procurement, public-benefit obligations, or required approvals.**
4. **Entity-resolution vendors are evidence inputs, not sources of legal authority.** BrightQuery/OpenCorporates are reference connectors, not mandatory providers.
5. **Source of funds is multi-dimensional.** Provider domicile, vehicle domicile, remitting jurisdiction, beneficial/control information where required, settlement rail, destination, and economic-risk jurisdiction remain separate fields.
6. **ICSN JNR is canonical for technical jurisdiction resolution.** ENS-style names are aliases/adapters and do not create sovereign/government authority.
7. **M5Global indicators are research/economic-intelligence outputs.** The current index/opportunity layer is not a registered exchange or other regulated venue.
8. **Public transparency and privacy are both required.** Public, regulator/qualified-assurance, and private/protected planes must remain distinct.
9. **Authoritative external records control their legal domain.** TitleChain/M5 provides evidence, coordination, provenance, policy, and reconciliation.
10. **Every derived indicator needs methodology, source lineage, revision history, privacy threshold, confidence/verification state, and limitations.**
11. **Open/reference identifiers do not create authority.** A BrightQuery, OpenCorporates, OpenData.org, Overture/GERS, ENS/JNR, LEI/CIK or other identifier is a reference/evidence key whose legal significance depends on the authoritative source and current role.
12. **Source-chain and destination confirmation are distinct.** An activated source path does not itself prove destination receipt, lawful deployment, settlement finality, title change or investment performance.
13. **Analytics must separate fact from inference.** Jurisdiction flow correlations cannot be presented as motive, control, creditworthiness, project quality, government policy or investment suitability without separate evidence and lawful analytical methodology.
