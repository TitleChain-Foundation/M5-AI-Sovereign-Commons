## 2. Why this pilot exists

The project tests the TitleChain/M5 process on a public, federally owned, historically designated civic building — a materially different legal and operational path from an ordinary private real-estate transfer.

The project is designed to connect five layers without allowing any one layer to silently replace another:

1. **M5 participant accounts** — verified human, role, credential, jurisdiction, eligibility, and permission state.
2. **Legal + public authority** — deed, public owner, lease, permits, licenses, financing instruments, government authority, and executed agreements.
3. **AI-native banking + transaction operations** — investor, escrow, banking, permitting, procurement, payments, reporting, notices, and authorized workflow coordination.
4. **TitleChain + M5Canon evidence/rights graph** — asset provenance, ownership/stewardship, rights, restrictions, authority, state transitions, and receipts.
5. **Productive infrastructure** — energy, water, food, compute, communications, workspace, education, enterprise, and public services.

AI can assist with research, classification, drafting, routing, reconciliation, and workflow coordination. **AI does not create legal authority.**

## 3. SEC public-comment alignment — live implementation test

TitleChain Foundation filed a public comment with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on **September 5, 2026** in **File No. S7-2026-30 / Release No. 34-106246**, addressing the proposed modernization of transfer-agent rules. The filing recommends voluntary, implementation-neutral open standards for credential-bound human/entity authority, machine-readable transfer restrictions, bounded automated agents, preservation of underlying title/right provenance, and interoperable regulator-ready evidence.

**312 Spring Commons is proposed as a real-world implementation test of those recommendations.** It is not represented as SEC-approved, SEC-reviewed, or a regulatory sandbox. The purpose is to show, in public, how the same architecture can coordinate a federal-to-public asset transition, project capitalization, investment instruments, permitting, procurement, operating rights, productive infrastructure, and lifecycle evidence while leaving each government agency, registered intermediary, fiduciary, bank, professional, and public owner responsible for its own legally authoritative acts and records.

The implementation follows the same three-part separation emphasized in the SEC filing:

**authority → execution → evidence**

A wallet, M5 account, credential, API key, model, token, or blockchain event does not create legal authority by itself. The system must resolve the accountable entity, current authorized human, exact role/appointment/credential, jurisdiction, scope, restrictions, revocation state, required approvals, execution event, and authoritative resulting record.

### SEC recommendation → Spring Commons implementation

| SEC filing recommendation | Spring Commons implementation test |
|---|---|
| **Credential-bound participant identifiers** | M5BOM human root plus entity/role account, current appointment/credential, jurisdiction, scope, status, revocation, and evidence before consequential action |
| **Known entity → current human authority → bounded agent** | Government, issuer, investor, contractor, fiduciary, and regulated-provider entities are resolved first; authorized people second; AI/software delegation remains narrower and revocable |
| **Machine-readable restrictions** | Investment instruments and restricted rights can carry versioned policy references tied to legal source, eligibility, permitted routes, approval, and removal authority |
| **Ricardian representation** | Controlling human-readable terms + structured machine meaning + separately bounded executable instructions share identifiers/version/digest and a conflict rule |
| **Pre-activation agent controls** | Identity → role/credential → jurisdiction/legal context → deterministic policy → required approval → tamper-evident receipt |
| **Origination separate from later transfer authority** | Authority to create/register an asset or instrument does not automatically confer transfer, approval, correction, freeze, restriction-removal, or cancellation authority |
| **Underlying right preserved across wrappers/rails** | Physical title, leasehold, financing rights, securities, settlement rails, and digital representations remain distinct but linked |
| **Regulator-ready evidence** | Open documented export can reproduce actor, authority source, instruction, approvals, execution event, authoritative-record update, exceptions/corrections, and evidence |

**Regulatory boundary:** TitleChain Foundation, M5, TitleChain Registry, and the proposed Public Trust of America do not become a registered transfer agent, broker-dealer, clearing agency, exchange, custodian, bank, or government authority merely because the pilot records or coordinates those functions. Where a regulated function is required, an appropriately registered or authorized participant must be selected and onboarded.

## 4. Open capital provenance + jurisdictional flow graph

Spring Commons also tests whether the **source, authority, material terms, jurisdiction path, and use of capital can be made reconstructable without publishing protected private data**. The v0.6 package materially expands **DOC-23 — Source of Funds, Entity Provenance, Jurisdiction Chain & Public Transaction Graph Standard** with open entity/spatial grounding, source-chain activation, and M5Global intelligence controls.

The core rule is:

> **No material dollar, entity, authority, economic right, control right, conflict, restriction, or project obligation should become operative while remaining outside the transaction's disclosed authority and evidence graph.**

A lawful fund, SPV, trust, holding company, or intermediary can participate, but it is treated as a **node in the graph rather than an opacity wall**. Material side arrangements may remain confidential when legitimately required, but their existence, parties, classification, evidence reference, and material economic/control effect must not silently contradict the public deal architecture or bypass required approvals.

### Entity resolution + M5-CV

Before an entity receives a consequential M5 role, the project should resolve the legal entity, current authorized human, role/appointment, jurisdiction, current status, and required regulatory/professional credentials. **BrightQuery or equivalent** may be evaluated as a reference KYB/entity-intelligence connector, with **OpenCorporates or equivalent official-source legal-entity data** as an additional provenance source. **OpenData.org** can provide an open, freely downloadable entity-reference graph for public reproducibility, and **Overture Maps / GERS** can provide stable spatial/place identifiers for physical locations. These sources help resolve and connect entities; they do not create legal authority. Authoritative registries, regulators, agencies, professional bodies, executed instruments, banks, courts, and other controlling records remain authoritative.

As of September 2026, OpenData.org reports approximately **324 million organizations, 1.2 billion people, 512 million places, and 222 countries**, and identifies BrightQuery as an initiating member and seed-data provider. Coverage counts are dated source metadata, not a conformance requirement. Overture Maps Foundation announced BrightQuery as a General Member in February 2026 and is incorporating BrightQuery-contributed entity/location data into its open Places work.

### Jurisdictional Capital Provenance Path

Each material funding event should be able to preserve a **Jurisdictional Capital Provenance Path (M5-JCP-001)** rather than one simplistic “country of funds” label. Distinct fields can include ultimate source where review is required, fund/vehicle domicile, provider domicile, beneficial/control jurisdiction where required, remitting institution, currency/rail, project destination, governing legal pathway, and economic-risk jurisdiction.

A source chain becomes **`SOURCE_CHAIN_ACTIVATED` only after entity, authority, source-of-funds, jurisdiction-path, and regulatory-routing evidence reaches the required verified state**. The project separately records `DESTINATION_CHAIN_CONFIRMED` when destination-side authoritative evidence confirms where funds were legally received or deployed. Historical paths remain versioned rather than overwritten.

Illustrative only:

**Singapore provider → Delaware vehicle → United Kingdom remitting institution → USD regulated bank rail → California / Spring Commons project destination.**

The public view may show privacy-safe source-to-destination flows while protected KYC/KYB, bank, beneficial-owner, source-of-wealth, and security-sensitive evidence remains in the regulator/qualified-assurance or private plane.

### JNR + technical chain aliases

The **ICSN Jurisdiction Naming & Resolution Registry (JNR)** provides the canonical jurisdiction layer. Optional aliases such as `singaporechain.eth`, `japanchain.eth`, `canadachain.eth`, `unitedkingdomchain.eth`, `unitedstateschain.eth`, or state-level aliases may be used as technical adapters only if actually registered and mapped.


**Capital-provenance guide:** Start with the human-readable [schema guide](./schemas/README.md) and [example guide](./examples/README.md). Technical users can continue from those pages to the raw JSON Schema and synthetic JSON record. These are draft reference artifacts for public review and do not evidence a real capital event.
**A `.eth` name does not establish government sponsorship, delegated authority, sovereignty, or control of an official government domain.** The canonical JNR record and authoritative jurisdiction evidence control.

### M5Global capital-flow intelligence

Privacy-protected, source-linked project evidence may support a research layer such as a proposed **M5 Global Capital Flow Index (M5-GCFI)** and an **M5 Opportunity Registry** showing where projects, capital needs, and lawful participation opportunities are emerging across jurisdictions. Open entity and spatial reference graphs can make those records easier to join and reproduce across systems: M5 canonical IDs can crosswalk to official registration numbers, LEIs/CIKs where applicable, BrightQuery keys, OpenCorporates mappings, OpenData.org reference IDs, and Overture GERS place IDs. Derived indicators must publish methodology, source lineage, dataset/version metadata, coverage period, verification/confidence state, revision history, privacy thresholds, conflict controls, limitations, and methodology version.

The broader architecture may use the future concept name **M5 Global Index and Exchange**, but the current index/intelligence layer is **not** represented as a registered securities exchange, ATS, broker-dealer, transfer agent, custodian, clearing agency, bank, or other regulated venue. Any future regulated matching, trading, custody, clearing, settlement, or transfer function would require the actual registrations, approvals, exemptions, and authorized operators applicable to that activity.

## 8. TitleChain asset passports + data governance

If selected and deployed, major equipment can receive a persistent TitleChain asset identifier and versioned **asset passport** documenting provenance, ownership or stewardship, authorized use, maintenance, financing restrictions, measured operating evidence, and end-of-life responsibility.

Example assets include solar modules, inverters, batteries, water pumps, greenhouse equipment, refurbished compute nodes, storage devices, network equipment, sensors, and recovered components.

A PV-module passport may record manufacturer/origin, model and serial, installation/commissioning date, inverter association, output history, maintenance, warranty, financing/stewardship state, repair/take-back, and end-of-life pathway.

A circular-compute passport may record lawful transfer, hardware/component identifiers, data sanitization, refurbishment/testing, workload class, energy profile, thermal-recovery linkage, repair state, and final reuse/harvest/recycling disposition.

### Privacy rule

Public transparency does **not** mean publishing private or security-sensitive data. The architecture is intended to keep private communications, identity documents, biometric/health information, passwords, private keys, precise personal location history, network topology, private workloads, and sensitive infrastructure details out of immutable public records. Aggregate energy, water, food, compute, circularity, and uptime metrics can be published while restricted evidence remains permissioned.

## 9. M5 Account First

Every participant begins with a verified human principal before project access.

### M5BOM — Bank of Me

**M5BOM** is the proposed root account for the verified credentialed human. It establishes the human principal who controls the account and anchors the roles, entities, and agents that person is authorized to represent.

### Credentialed role accounts

- **M5BOB — Bank of Business:** business, vendor, contractor, operator, investor entity, or other commercial capacity.
- **M5BOI — Bank of Institution:** nonprofit, foundation, university, financial institution, or other institutional capacity.
- **M5BOG — Bank of Government:** authorized government/public-entity capacity and delegated role.
- **M5BOU — Bank of Us:** proposed collective/member layer for eligible participation in the Public Trust of America commons network.

The architecture separates **the human from the role**. A person may control or represent more than one authorized capacity, but actions for a company, institution, or government body occur through the applicable credentialed role.

**M5 does not itself create a license, investor status, government authority, legal membership right, or financial entitlement.** Required KYC/KYB, investor verification, licensing, public delegation, procurement qualification, tax status, or other regulated determinations must come from the applicable authoritative source.

### Authority + Responsibility Registry

Before a government, regulated institution, professional, fiduciary, investor, contractor, or automated agent can perform a consequential project action, the project should resolve and record:

- canonical legal entity or institution;
- authoritative source of authority — statute, regulation, deed program, appointment, license, registration, resolution, contract, board action, delegation, or other controlling source;
- exact office/role and accountable human;
- jurisdiction and transaction/function scope;
- current credential, registration, appointment, or good-standing evidence where required;
- expiration and revocation state;
- approval threshold and required co-approvals;
- authoritative system of record; and
- evidence receipt for proposed, approved, rejected, executed, corrected, or reversed actions.

Candidate offices such as the **California Governor's Office, California Attorney General, Los Angeles Mayor's Office, City Council/County Board, City Attorney/County Counsel, and permitting departments should be onboarded only if the final legal pathway assigns them an actual approval, oversight, fiduciary, notice, or signing function.** The project should not infer signing authority from an office title.

For the Historic Surplus working pathway, the project should continue to model fee title as a conveyance to a **qualifying public entity**, with GSA and NPS roles determined by the applicable federal process. The proposed Public Trust of America may serve as a stewardship, membership, leasing, operating, or program framework only to the extent permitted by the final conveyance, lease, governing documents, and approvals; the documents should not imply a direct federal fee-title conveyance to the Trust unless a lawful eligibility pathway is separately established.

## 11. AI-native banking + transaction operating layer

A project of this scale does not move through one institution. It requires investors, foundations, lenders, banks, escrow providers, tax-credit participants, public owners, city agencies, permitting authorities, contractors, operators, insurers, auditors, and community stakeholders to coordinate many actions across separate systems.

The proposed transaction operating layer provides a common trusted state showing:

**who the participant is → what authority/credential they hold → what they may do → what document or capital obligation applies → what approval is required → what occurred → what happens next**

The system is intended as a **connector, not a closed replacement** for banks, governments, permitting systems, transfer agents, accounting platforms, insurers, escrow providers, or other systems of record.

### Regulated capital + market participants

The capital layer should select participants by **function**, not by brand. Depending on the final instrument and pathway, roles may include the issuer/borrower or project vehicle, a registered transfer agent, broker-dealer or placement agent, ATS or other lawful venue, custodian, DTC/DTCC participant or settlement provider, bank/escrow provider, CPA/auditor, tax professional, counsel, trustee/fiduciary, and other licensed or registered service providers.

A role is activated only when the transaction actually uses it and the participant's current registration, license, appointment, scope, and authority are verified. In particular, a registered transfer agent — if the selected security requires or uses one — remains accountable for the official Master Securityholder File and regulated transfer-agent functions. TitleChain may preserve linked provenance and evidence but does not supersede the transfer agent's official books. Likewise, DTC/DTCC infrastructure should be referenced only if the selected security/settlement/custody structure actually uses it.

Illustrative investor flow:

**qualification → diligence → instrument → commitment/subscription → escrow → milestone draw → asset/milestone evidence → reconciliation → reporting → repayment/distribution under definitive documents**

Illustrative public workflow:

**authorized agency identity → application/evidence → fees → review → inspection → permit/status event → signed notice/receipt**

## 13. Public, auditable metrics

The project is intended to measure actual performance rather than rely on generic sustainability claims.

- **Energy:** on-site generation, grid import/export, battery state, peak reduction, critical-load coverage, islanded hours.
- **Water:** condensate/rainwater captured, treated/reused volume, water-quality results, AWG kWh/gallon if used.
- **Food:** output, production area, water use, energy use, cycle time, and disposition.
- **Compute:** node uptime, kWh, renewable share, interruptible workload hours, useful recovered heat.
- **Circularity:** second-life hardware rate, repair/reuse, component harvest, certified disposition.
- **Economic:** utility savings, demand response, compute/food/program revenue, maintenance cost, reserve contribution.
- **Community:** workforce hours, credentials earned, local procurement, service availability, and participation.

## 16. What this is and isn't

- The project remains a **simulation and diligence package**, not a completed transaction.
- Publication does not create an investment, account, membership right, credential, government authority, legal representation, vendor award, or entitlement.
- A TitleChain record is evidence/coordination infrastructure; it does not automatically override deeds, property law, security interests, procurement contracts, warranties, building codes, food/water rules, utility tariffs, privacy law, or authoritative government records.
- M5BOU participation does not automatically grant rights in every future commons.
- Candidate technologies are examples, not selected vendors.
- The project may cite the TitleChain Foundation SEC public comment as the source of the reference architecture, but must not imply that the SEC endorsed, approved, certified, or adopted the Spring Commons implementation.
- Physical federal-property conveyance and securities/investment-instrument transfer are separate legal record domains even when TitleChain links their evidence.
- Productive-infrastructure benchmarks are targets to be measured, not guarantees.
