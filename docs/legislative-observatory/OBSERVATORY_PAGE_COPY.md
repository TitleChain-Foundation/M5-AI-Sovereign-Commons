# Proposed public page copy (draft for editorial approval)

## Legislative & Regulatory Observatory
### Read the record. Examine the text. Participate in public decisions.

The TitleChain Foundation Legislative & Regulatory Observatory brings together official government records, Foundation model legislation, independent analysis and public participation resources. Each record identifies its source, date, legislative status and outstanding questions. A Foundation proposal is not an enacted law, and Foundation analysis is not a government finding.

### Sovereign Compute Access Act
**Status: Foundation / ICSN model legislation — Draft v2, revised November 18, 2026. Not introduced or enacted.**

The proposed Sovereign Compute Access Act addresses private access to open-weight AI, individual and organizational authority over AI agents, vendor-neutral verification, registered sovereign compute nodes and state-primary administration. The Foundation invites technical, legal and public review of the actual text before legislative sponsorship.

**Read the canonical draft:** https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/legislation/sovereign-compute-access-act/OFFICIAL-TEXT.md

**Technical companion:** https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/rfcs/0002-proof-of-control-legal-entity-agent-profile.md

The November 18 revision states that it adds agent-verification provisions to sections 2, 3, 105, 203, 302 and 304. Do not assert that additional Section 205 identity provisions are in this version without confirming the text or approved successor.

### H.R. 2347 / Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act
**Status: Passed the U.S. Senate as amended on November 19, 2026; latest House disposition requires verification at release.**

H.R. 2347 originated as survivor-related tax legislation and became the Senate legislative vehicle for a broader permitting and infrastructure measure. The Observatory will preserve the distinction between the original House bill, the Senate substitute, subsequent amendments and the Foundation's analysis. It will examine effects on public lands, environmental review, infrastructure and the survivor tax provisions without conflating those subjects.

**Official Senate passage vote:** https://www.senate.gov/legislative/LIS/roll_call_votes/vote1192/vote_119_2_00270.htm

**Congressional Record, November 19:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2026-11-19/html/CREC-2026-11-19-pt1-PgS5797-2.htm

**Official legislative record:** https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/2347

### Participate
Read the primary sources. Identify your representatives and the responsible committees. Submit comments through an official government channel when one is available, or share research and technical feedback with the Foundation for its public review process.

**Find your representative:** https://www.house.gov/representatives/find-your-representative

**Find senators:** https://www.senate.gov/senators/senators-contact.htm

**Important:** Submitting feedback to the Foundation is not the same as filing an official congressional or regulatory comment. Do not enter sensitive identity, medical, financial or private credential information in public submissions.
