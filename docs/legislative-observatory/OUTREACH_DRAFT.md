## TitleChain Foundation opens a public legislative review

Congress is making decisions that shape how Americans use technology, develop infrastructure and protect public resources. The public should be able to read the actual record and see what changed.

The TitleChain Foundation Legislative & Regulatory Observatory will bring official legislative records, proposed reforms and public review into one accessible place. We are beginning with the Foundation's proposed Sovereign Compute Access Act and the Senate-amended H.R. 2347, including its permitting provisions and survivor-related tax relief.

Our proposed Sovereign Compute Access Act has **not** been introduced in Congress. It is a model bill open for review. H.R. 2347 passed the Senate on November 19, 2026, by 62–33; Senate passage is not enactment.

We invite legislative counsel, researchers, technical standards contributors, community organizations and members of the public to examine the sources, identify errors and contribute constructive review. We will distinguish official records from Foundation recommendations and publish corrections with provenance.

**Read the record. Ask questions. Help improve the proposal.**
