# H.R. 2347 — Legislative and Observatory action sequence

**Scope: Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act only.** No other divisions of the Senate substitute are included in the proposed House amendment.

1. **Freeze evidence.** Preserve the original House-engrossed H.R. 2347, SA 6883 (Nov. 10), the actual Senate-passed version, the House/Senate action histories and roll calls. Record URL, version, retrieval date, SHA-256 of downloaded bytes, and any provenance gaps. Do not represent a source URL alone as a verified local hash.
2. **Verify Senate-passed text.** The uploaded SenateBill.pdf is the proposed amendment text. Confirm any changes between SA 6883 as proposed and the exact amendment approved and bill passed before claiming identical final language.
3. **Request House Legislative Counsel review.** Confirm the proposed motion: “That the House concur in the amendment of the Senate to H.R. 2347 with an amendment,” and replacement text that restores only original H.R. 2347 §§1–2. Confirm the appropriate formal amendment formatting and engrossment conventions.
4. **Identify a House floor pathway.** An incumbent House member may request action; House leadership/Rules Committee may arrange consideration. Determine whether a special rule, unanimous consent, or other available procedure is needed. The Speaker alone cannot enact or guarantee consideration.
5. **House action.** If formally considered, record the motion, amendment, rule, debate, roll-call vote, and resulting engrossed House amendment. A proposal is not a vote; a position statement is not sponsorship.
6. **Return to Senate.** If the House adopts its further amendment, the Senate must concur in that exact amendment or negotiate further. Earlier Senate approval of a broader substitute does not count as concurrence in the narrowed House amendment.
7. **Presentment.** When both chambers agree to identical text, the enrolled measure may be presented to the President for constitutional action.
8. **Session deadline.** If the 119th Congress adjourns sine die without enactment, the measure does not carry over to the 120th Congress; it must be reintroduced.
9. **Public statements.** Members-elect can publicly state positions now, but cannot cast House votes until taking office. Log statements with exact source and date, without assigning unverified positions.
10. **Observatory release controls.** Separate source facts, PAINE commentary, draft legislative language, and campaign visuals. Require human legal review before marking the amendment ready for official filing; require explicit project approval before merging or deploying.
