# Drafting note addendum — congressional precedents

**For House Legislative Counsel and parliamentary review · November 23, 2026**

## Form of the House motion and amendment

**H.R. 5009 (118th Congress)** is the closest recent model for the form. On December 11, 2024, the House considered a motion to concur in the Senate amendment with a further House amendment consisting of Rules Committee Print 118–52. The House amendment used replacement language directed at the matter the Senate proposed to insert. The Senate agreed to the House amendment, and the measure became Public Law 118–159 on December 23, 2024.

- [House motion and amendment text](https://www.congress.gov/118/crec/2024/12/11/170/184/CREC-2024-12-11-pt1-PgH6827.pdf)
- [Senate agreement communicated to the House](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2024-12-18/pdf/CREC-2024-12-18-pt1-PgH7348.pdf)
- [Public Law 118–159](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-118publ159)

**H.R. 3547 (113th Congress)** provides a second enacted example and an engrossed House amendment specimen. The House agreed to the Senate amendment to the bill text with a replacement amendment on January 15, 2014. The Senate concurred on January 16, and the bill became Public Law 113–76 on January 17.

- [House-engrossed amendment](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/BILLS-113hr3547eah)
- [Public Law 113–76 and legislative history](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-113publ76)

The recovered H.R. 2347 draft already follows this form. It pairs a **motion to concur with an amendment** with a separate **House amendment to Senate amendment** that replaces the matter the Senate proposed to insert with the survivor-only text. For the official version, ask the Clerk and Parliamentarian to confirm whether the Senate message is available for House consideration, how to identify the precise amendment to the text, and whether a separate title amendment must be disposed of. Confirm germaneness, any applicable points of order, and whether a special rule or other floor procedure is needed. The earlier bills establish a form and sequence. H.R. 2347 requires its own procedural determination and Senate agreement to the House text.

- [119th Congress House Manual, Jefferson’s Manual on amendments between the Houses](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/HMAN-119/html/HMAN-119-jeffersonman.htm)

## Tax drafting analogues

- **IRC §139F, added by the PATH Act of 2015, §304.** Congress excluded specified civil damages, restitution, and other monetary awards tied to wrongful incarceration. This is a policy analogue for excluding compensation for a defined harm. Its eligibility, payment categories, and transition rules are specific to wrongful incarceration. [IRS explanation and examples](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/wrongful-incarceration-faqs)
- **Public Law 115–97, §13307, enacted as IRC §162(q).** Congress addressed tax treatment of certain sexual-harassment or abuse settlements through a deduction restriction tied to nondisclosure agreements. The provision operates on deductions and supplies no recipient income exclusion. Its terminology addresses a different scope from the House-engrossed H.R. 2347 definitions. [Enacted text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-131/pdf/STATUTE-131-Pg2054.pdf)

Use **House-engrossed H.R. 2347 §§1–2** as the source for the operative substitute. Before filing, have counsel compare it against Senate-engrossed §3001 and current Code text. Confirm the exact §104(a)(2) insertion and §104(d) redesignation, the limited §7491(a) credible-evidence and burden-of-proof provisions, the 18 U.S.C. §2246 definitions, the punitive-damages exception, and the decision and settlement effective dates. Confirm whether an updated revenue estimate or offset analysis is needed for floor consideration.
