2. **Parliamentary posture — BLOCKER.** Confirm that the House is in possession of the Senate message and that no intervening House action has changed the stage of disagreement. Formal wording depends on the exact pending Senate amendment(s). Consult House Parliamentarian and House Legislative Counsel.
3. **Motion form — REVIEW REQUIRED.** Proposed 'That the House concur in the amendment of the Senate to H.R. 2347 with an amendment' is a recognizable motion type, but do not call it filing-ready. Legislative counsel should validate the amendment's instruction ('In lieu of the matter proposed to be inserted ...') against the precise Senate amendment and drafting conventions.
4. **House floor route — BLOCKER.** Senate amendments to House bills generally are not privileged for immediate House consideration. Establish actual floor access via a Rules Committee special rule, motion to suspend the rules where permitted (two-thirds required), or unanimous consent. Check germaneness, waivers, notice, and applicable timing.
5. **Senate agreement — BLOCKER.** Earlier Senate approval of a broad substitute is not concurrence in a survivor-only House amendment. The Senate must act anew, and debate/cloture or unanimous-consent issues may arise.
6. **Enrolled text — REVIEW REQUIRED.** Only identical language approved by both houses can be enrolled and presented to the President. Verify text at engrossment/enrollment; confirm title and section renumbering.
7. **Substantive copy — REVIEW REQUIRED.** The draft has normalized typography and formatting; validate word for word against the House-engrossed text, including quotation nesting, em dashes, punctuation and statutory cross-references. No machine-certified exact diff is currently included.
