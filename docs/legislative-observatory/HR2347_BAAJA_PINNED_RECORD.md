# H.R. 2347 — From the Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act to BAAJA

**Record type:** congressional bill version history and Foundation annotation.  
**Bill number:** H.R. 2347 (119th Congress; unchanged).  
**Original House legislation:** Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act.  
**Senate substitute:** Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026 (BAAJA).  
**Senate action:** passed amended bill November 19, 2026, 62–33 (five not voting).  
**Enactment:** not established by the cited Senate vote; check the latest official record before publication.

## Pinned survivor provision

**Division C, Section 3001 — survivor tax relief.** The Senate substitute retains survivor tax-relief provisions under this section according to the current comparison baseline. **Verification required:** pin the exact Senate-passed text, including all adopted floor amendments, before reproducing clause text or asserting exact equivalence with the original House version.

## What happened

The House-passed H.R. 2347 addressed tax treatment of certain damages received by survivors. The Senate used the same H.R. 2347 legislative vehicle for a much broader substitute called BAAJA. The official original and substitute should remain separately accessible. This is a change in the bill's substance and short title, **not a change to the H.R. 2347 number**.
