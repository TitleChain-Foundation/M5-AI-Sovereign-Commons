# People's Trust Public Conformance Pilot

**Status:** Proposed Pilot for Public Comment  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Reviewed:** September 7, 2026

## Purpose

The proposed People's Trust public pilot gives reviewers a concrete
agricultural-asset scenario for testing the M5 standards. It is intended to
show whether recurring title, identity, entity, authority, restriction,
recordkeeping, correction, and portability requirements can be represented as
distinct, inspectable, and reusable evidence before an action executes.

The pilot begins with synthetic records. It is not:

- an acquisition announcement;
- an offer or solicitation to buy or sell a security;
- evidence that a seller, owner, operator, regulator, transfer agent, or public
  agency has agreed to participate;
- a statement that People's Trust owns or controls a particular asset;
- an SEC, state, county, or other governmental approval;
- a production deployment or legal-compliance certification.

## Demonstration thesis

> Classification and authority must be resolved before execution. Possession of
> a token, wallet, key, account, credential, or agent does not create title,
> professional standing, regulated status, or permission.

The pilot tests whether separate authoritative systems can interoperate without
being confused with one another.

## Synthetic agricultural scenario

The public test fixture models an agricultural project with these independent
layers:

1. **Underlying asset and title** — the official real-property record,
   encumbrances, and separately held rights.
2. **Stewardship** — mission, conservation, community, and anti-capture
   governance that does not itself convey title or economic rights.
3. **Local operations** — a qualified operator with bounded operational
   authority and budget.
4. **Entity and signer authority** — lawful entities, current roles, governing
   instruments, approvals, delegation, and revocation.
5. **Economic instrument** — a separately analyzed right, if any, with explicit
   issuer, terms, classification, holder eligibility, and restrictions.
6. **Regulated recordkeeping** — one accountable recordkeeping transfer agent
   where securities transfer-agent regulation applies.
7. **Evidence and correction** — attributable receipts, rejected actions,
   exceptions, corrections, supersession, and successor portability.

The fixture keeps fee title, leases, crops, water, mineral rights, operations,
stewardship membership, and economic interests distinct.

## Program structure

The intended program is not one aggregated corporation, token, wallet, or
balance sheet. It is a permanent public-benefit stewardship layer connected to
individually identifiable projects.

```text
permanent stewardship commons
        |
        +-- mission, anti-capture rules, and public standards
        +-- one-human/one-vote governance where adopted
        +-- no automatic title, operating, or distribution right
        |
        v
versioned project registry
        |
        +-- project title or asset layer
        +-- qualified local operating layer
        +-- separately bounded acquisition and operating accounts
        +-- stewardship and resilience reserve
        +-- separately analyzed economic vehicle, if any
        +-- accountable transfer-agent workflow where required
        +-- public-safe evidence and conformance record
```

Each project remains legally, economically, operationally, and evidentially
distinguishable. Capital or economic participation in one project does not
purchase greater stewardship control over the commons or create authority over
another project.

The local operator controls ordinary operations only within the verified
operating agreement, budget, credentials, and prohibited-action boundaries.
The operator does not thereby receive authority to convey title, issue an
instrument, move participant interests, or use another project's funds.

## Bounded financial architecture

Where legally permitted and separately approved, a live project may use
distinct accounts for:

- acquisition and closing;
- title, escrow, tax, filing, and governmental fees;
- local operations, payroll, equipment, insurance, and approved inputs;
- soil, water, capital-improvement, emergency, and resilience reserves;
- a separately lawful economic vehicle and its authorized funds.

No personal wallet, agent wallet, stewardship account, or general program
treasury should silently become the beneficial owner or unrestricted controller
of project funds. Every account requires a declared owner, purpose, authorized
actors, limits, approval thresholds, prohibited actions, reconciliation source,
and revocation pathway.

## Authority sequence

For a regulated transfer or other consequential action:

```text
external legal and regulatory status
        |
        v
verified accountable entity and human operator
        |
        v
current role, credential, delegation, and execution binding
        |
        v
instrument, jurisdiction, holder, and restriction evaluation
        |
        v
required accountable approval
        |
        v
M5Canon ALLOW or DENY
        |
        v
bounded execution and tamper-evident receipt
```

If a required regulated role, registration, credential, delegation, approval,
or authoritative source is missing, stale, revoked, disputed, or out of scope,
the regulated function is not executable.

An M5 credential represents verified evidence about authority established
elsewhere. It does not create a license or governmental status.

## SEC public-comment context

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission published the proposed
**Transfer Agent Rules**, Release No. **34-106246**, File No. **S7-2026-30**, in
the Federal Register at **91 FR 56946** on September 4, 2026. The official
Federal Register record states that comments should be received on or before
**November 3, 2026**.

Official record:
<https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/04/2026-18190/transfer-agent-rules>

The proposal concerns modernization of transfer-agent rules, registration and
reporting forms, and electronic records and operations. This pilot is offered
as an independent public-review scenario. It is not sponsored, approved, or
endorsed by the SEC.

These are the pilot's questions, not questions attributed to the Commission:

- authoritative master securityholder records and linked systems;
- wallet-to-person or wallet-to-entity association without unnecessary public
  disclosure;
- electronic instructions, receipt, acceptance, rejection, and posting;
- operational capacity, service providers, and dependencies;
- immutable events versus legally effective corrections;
- transfer restrictions and their authoritative legal sources;
- safeguards, incident response, auditability, and retention;
- examination exports and successor transfer-agent portability;
- technology-neutral conformance tests.

The public repository does not submit a formal SEC comment. Any formal filing
requires a separately approved document, counsel review, current deadline
verification, and submission through an official channel.

## Evidence and claim states

Every public project claim **MUST** be labeled:

- `SYNTHETIC` — test-only data;
- `PROPOSED` — a design or intended action;
- `UNVERIFIED_LEAD` — research requiring authoritative confirmation;
- `VERIFIED_SOURCE` — supported by a cited authoritative record;
- `UNDER_REVIEW` — evidence exists but required review is incomplete;
- `AUTHORIZED` — the identified accountable authority approved the stated
  scope;
- `ACTIVE` — independently evidenced operation within the approved scope;
- `CORRECTED` or `SUPERSEDED` — retained with an attributable change record;
- `DO_NOT_PUBLISH` — private, privileged, personal, security-sensitive, or
  unsupported material.

`VERIFIED_SOURCE` does not necessarily mean legally sufficient, current,
complete, transferable, approved, or authorized.

## Activation gates for a live candidate

A named asset cannot be presented as an active acquisition, offering, or
regulated pilot until applicable gates pass:

1. exact asset and scope;
2. current authoritative title;
3. lawful entities, standing, control, and signers;
4. applicable jurisdiction and official sources;
5. seller or issuer authority and documented willingness;
6. qualified operator and operating authority;
7. title, water, lease, environmental, tax, and other diligence;
8. valuation, financing, budgets, reserves, and money-flow controls;
9. instrument characterization and offering pathway, if any;
10. required transfer agent, broker, portal, custodian, escrow, or other
    regulated roles;
11. privacy, security, incident-response, and evidence controls;
12. counsel and accountable governance approval;
13. reproducible conformance, correction, and successor-export results.

Research concerning named properties, owners, operators, prices, or possible
transactions remains outside the public Commons until these claim gates permit
the exact statement.

Historical rights reconstruction may cite already-public archival records only
after source and disclosure review. It must not identify a living private
individual or connect an archival record to a current nonpublic candidate unless
the exact disclosure has separately passed the applicable claim gates.

## Non-disclosure boundary

Before a separately approved announcement, public materials **MUST NOT**
contain or encode:

- property, project, owner, seller, operator, trust, company, manager, counsel,
  or beneficial-owner names;
- street addresses, parcel numbers, legal descriptions, exact acreage, maps,
  photographs, or coordinates that identify the candidate;
- purchase price, valuation, term sheet, financing source, seller contact, or
  negotiation status;
- private title, lease, water, environmental, operational, financial, or legal
  records;
- names embedded in file metadata, image alternative text, URLs, branch names,
  commit messages, schema examples, receipts, or test fixtures.

Public examples should use reserved synthetic identifiers and clearly fictional
values. Aggregate descriptions may be detailed enough to explain the control
problem even if an informed reader can infer a possible region or use case.
Inference is not confirmation, and maintainers must not validate speculation.

Visual redaction **MUST NOT** be implemented by hiding the real text with CSS,
black highlighting, blur, overlays, collapsible elements, comments, accessible
labels, transparent images, or client-side scripts. The underlying public file
must contain only the generic block label and public-safe text.

## Public conformance scenarios

The initial synthetic suite should test:

- invalid wallet ownership presented as legal authority;
- expired or revoked professional credentials;
- wrong signer or entity capacity;
- title, operator, crop, water, and economic rights incorrectly collapsed;
- unverified instrument classification;
- ineligible recipient or prohibited transfer;
- missing required transfer agent;
- conflicting onchain and master-file state;
- rejected instruction and attempted-action evidence;
- correction without deletion of historical evidence;
- provider, chain, wallet, or transfer-agent replacement;
- public/private evidence separation;
- interrupted operation and successor export.

## Measures

The pilot should report:

- time to verify entity, signer, and role status;
- time to assemble an authoritative-source inventory;
- percentage of required fields supported by authoritative evidence;
- number of unresolved or conflicting claims;
- rejected invalid actions detected before execution;
- time to reproduce the current authoritative state;
- time to produce an authorized examination package;
- portability and successor-import results;
- privacy findings and unnecessary data exposure;
- controls reusable across a second independent scenario.

Faster completion is not success if authority, privacy, security, legal review,
or evidence quality is reduced.
