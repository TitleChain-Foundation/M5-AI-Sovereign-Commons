category is the program lane; the named project is the current public simulation

under the relevant lane without collapsing or renaming the three foundational
categories.

## Cross-cutting public-review layers

The categories do not replace the Commons-wide review layers. All three may
reference, where relevant:

- proposed Commons standards and schemas;
- jurisdiction and authority evidence;
- TitleChain provenance and authoritative-record separation;
- SEC public input / transfer-agent modernization research;
- conformance and failure testing;
- SHADOW public-research indexing;
- privacy and public/regulator/private evidence separation.

A cross-cutting layer does not make every project subject to the same law.
Jurisdiction and regulated-function analysis remains project- and instrument-specific.
