# Canonical Public Activation Links

**Reviewed:** September 9, 2026

Use this registry to prevent future or internal activation stages from being
presented as live public pathways.

| Public starting action | Approved labels | Canonical URL | Current check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Set up the free IAM account | `Set up your IAM account` | <https://m5bank.app/> | HTTP 200 |
| Join the public waitlist | `Join the waitlist`, `Reserve your M5POD`, or `Activate your spot` | <https://m5podactivationdemo.netlify.app/> | HTTP 200 |

These are the only current public IAM/activation pathway destinations. This
rule applies to enrollment, M5-CV, Member Passport, M5POD, and cohort CTAs.
Non-activation links to Commons documents, pilots, public discussions, security
reporting, and sponsorship may continue to point to their maintained
destinations.

## Future controlled stages

Do not publish another activation or enrollment URL until the accountable owner
confirms that the stage is live. Before publication, document who may use it,
whether intake is open, what information is collected, what notice and consent
apply, who approves activation, and why submission is not automatic admission,
credential issuance, or project authority.

Do not use another implementation endpoint in current public activation copy.
Historical and internal operational records may retain a URL when needed for
provenance, but must not present it as a live public CTA.
