## Objective

Activate local and professional participants as evidence contributors without turning contribution into authority.

| Participant | Useful contribution | Authority boundary |
|---|---|---|
| County recorder/clerk | recorded instruments, indexes, official copies | official records control according to applicable law |
| Title company | title search, exceptions, chain/encumbrance work | report/insurance scope controls; not government title creation |
| Notary | notarial act evidence | only within commissioned authority and applicable law |
| Assessor | parcel/assessment records | assessment record is not necessarily title |
| Government disposition office | inventory, status, disposition evidence | agency authority and official records control |
| Transfer agent | securityholder/transfer records | registered TA responsibility where applicable |
| Bank/escrow/payment provider | account/settlement evidence | provider's authoritative records control |
| Licensed professional | scoped attestation | credential, jurisdiction and engagement scope control |
| Researcher/citizen | public-source discovery and challenge | submission is not canonical authority |

## Evidence path

`DISCOVERED -> SUBMITTED -> SOURCE_VERIFIED -> AUTHORITY_VERIFIED -> REVIEWED -> CANONICAL_WHERE_APPLICABLE`

Exception states: `DISPUTED`, `CORRECTED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `REJECTED`.

Every contribution should preserve contributor/capacity, source, jurisdiction, document type, evidence class, timestamp, version, signature/proof if applicable, linked asset/event, review status, and dispute/supersession state.
