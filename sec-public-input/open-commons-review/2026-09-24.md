# People's Public Trust — Open Commons Review

**Status:** Proposed public-interest orientation for repository-owner review\
**Review period:** September 16–24, 2026\
**Prepared:** September 24, 2026\
**Official source:** SEC File No. S7-2026-30 / Release No. 34-106246

## Bottom line

This review period moves the Commons from a primarily abstract transfer-agent
discussion into a more concrete implementation question: can the same
authoritative-record, human-authority, restriction, correction and portability
principles be tested against real-world assets and financing structures without
turning the Commons itself into the bank, transfer agent, government record,
title office, exchange or regulator?

The Spring Commons simulation provides the first bounded test. It begins with a
real public asset and asks how federal disposition evidence, public title,
historic-use restrictions, project capitalization, bank/escrow movement,
investment instruments, regulated recordkeeping, local operating rights and
TitleChain evidence can remain separate but reconcilable.

The Public Bank of California concept adds a useful stress test because it forces
the architecture to distinguish a **public-capital concept** from an actually
chartered bank and from securities recordkeeping. That distinction is material:
modeling a financing layer does not authorize deposits, banking, custody,
securities transfers, public appropriations or government action.

This is Foundation public-interest analysis, not an SEC finding, rule
interpretation, endorsement, or additional SEC submission. The official SEC
proposal, docket, authoritative law and the records of responsible regulated
institutions control.

## What this implementation work adds

### 1. Start with the asset and the authoritative record

Spring Commons begins with the building, public ownership, disposition pathway,
jurisdiction and restrictions rather than beginning with a token or financing
wrapper.

**Open-commons test:** can every later financial or digital representation point
back to the authoritative asset/right record without silently replacing it?

### 2. Public capital does not collapse banking and securities functions

The Public Bank of California is being modeled only as a concept layer. A lawful
future public bank, public-finance institution, community bank, credit union,
escrow provider, securities issuer and transfer agent would each retain the
duties and authoritative records assigned to it by law and contract.

**Open-commons test:** can the transaction graph show who controls each record
and prove reconciliation among bank/escrow, title, issuer and transfer-agent
records without making TitleChain the source of legal authority?

### 3. A community-facing project still requires exact authority

Public purpose does not substitute for legal authority. The federal agency,
eligible public grantee, governing body, bank/escrow institution, regulated
intermediary, professional, operator and project signatory each require their
own current authority source.

**Open-commons test:** can a workflow fail closed when the office, signer,
license, appointment, approval, delegation or record source is missing, stale or
revoked?

### 4. Capital provenance should be reconstructable without publishing protected data

Spring Commons now tests a jurisdictional capital-provenance path that can
separate provider identity, vehicle domicile, remitting institution, settlement
rail, project destination and economic-right record while protecting KYC/KYB,
bank, beneficial-owner and other sensitive evidence.

**Open-commons test:** what is the minimum regulator-ready evidence needed to
reconstruct source, authority, use, restrictions and corrections without
publishing private records?

### 5. The Commons now has three development lanes, not one transaction thesis

The public navigation is being separated into:

1. **GSA / CRE** — 312 Spring Commons;
2. **FARMLAND / REDEVELOPMENT** — America's People's Trust Farmland; and
3. **GLOBAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECT** — Global UN Commons.

The same authority/evidence principles may be tested across all three, but the
legal rules are not assumed to be identical. Global UN Commons in particular is
not treated as subject to U.S. securities law merely because the Commons studies
interoperability.

**Open-commons test:** which control requirements are genuinely portable and
which must remain jurisdiction- or instrument-specific?

### 6. Every project answers the same control questions

A common Project Control Pack now asks each lane the same eight questions:
asset and source of truth; authority, jurisdiction and capital provenance;
instrument and regulatory routing; participant eligibility; escrow, treasury
and fiduciary functions; transfer, restriction, correction and succession;
risk, disclosure and privacy; and state machine and tests. M5-CV holds reusable
evidence, while a project-specific Participation Passport holds the permission,
and WORK, SUPPORT and FINANCIAL PARTICIPATION remain separate. The anonymized
North Dakota reference-farm simulation (`AG-PILOT-001 / ND-FARM-SIM-001`) is the
first project to publish all eight controls end to end.

**Open-commons test:** can a credentialed person discover and apply for work,
offer support, or be assessed for a financial instrument without any step
silently creating ownership, investment, voting, signing or transfer authority?

## Relationship to the Foundation's September 5 SEC comment

The September 5 public comment remains the Foundation's submitted position. The
current implementation work carries forward its core themes:

- credential-bound authority;
- known entity → current human authority → bounded automation;
- machine-readable restrictions tied to legal source;
- Ricardian human/machine/executable separation;
- pre-execution control gates;
- origination separate from later transfer/correction authority;
- preservation of the underlying right across wrappers and rails; and
- regulator-ready correction, reconciliation and successor evidence.

The Spring/Public Bank work does **not** add a new SEC comment by itself.
Repository updates, GitHub comments and Open Commons Reviews remain public
Foundation research unless separately submitted through the SEC's official
comment channel.

## What should be tested next

1. Public-property title and restriction reconciliation after a modeled
   financing event.
2. Bank/escrow settlement evidence without copying protected bank records into
   the public graph.
3. Transfer-agent master-file reconciliation where an M4/security instrument is
   modeled.
4. Restriction placement, exception, removal, correction and successor transfer.
5. Revoked or stale signer/credential failure before any consequential action.
6. Source-chain activation versus destination confirmation.
7. Public/regulator/private evidence-plane separation.
8. Community-bank or credit-union participation without implying replacement by
   a public-bank concept.
9. Public Bank of California concept boundary tests: no deposit taking, no
   banking charter, no government agency status, no securities intermediary
   status and no automatic project authority.
10. Cross-project portability of the same authority/evidence controls across
    Spring, Farmland and Global UN Commons.
11. Overlapping SEC, CFTC, state securities and banking/escrow routing for one
    Farmland financing arrangement, using `ND-FARM-SIM-001`.
12. Scoped Participation Passport checks that fail closed when project,
    instrument, role, jurisdiction or expiry does not match.
