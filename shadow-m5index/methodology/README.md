# SHADOW M5Index methodology

## Operating principles

1. **Evidence before inference.** Locate a record before estimating its meaning.
2. **State before scoring.** Represent known, unknown, disputed, and stale elements independently.
3. **Provenance before automation.** Consequential states need source and supersession lineage.
4. **Authority before action.** No score, threat, model, account, or agent creates authority.
5. **Events, not overwrites.** Preserve prior states and corrections.
6. **Rights are separable.** Surface ownership does not imply every associated right.
7. **Obligations travel too.** Track liens, remediation, preservation, decommissioning, and successor exposure.
8. **Public and private stay separated.** Public SHADOW outputs exclude private M5POD evidence.

## State model

A TitleChain Asset State should independently represent identity, jurisdiction, provenance, title, rights, encumbrances, claims, obligations, environment, authority, transfer, dispute, verification, and evidence. A verified deed does not imply no liens, no separated rights, environmental clearance, or transfer eligibility.

State is derived from append-only events. Corrections supersede prior claims without deleting history.

## Disposition proof

The public-transition, transaction-proof, and ownership-resolution dimensions are separate:

- `P0`–`P8` describe disposition/development progression.
- `S0`–`S4` distinguish listing, auction, award, closing, and deed confirmation.
- `O0`–`O5` distinguish undisclosed buyer through ultimate-capital resolution.

Therefore:

- high bid $\ne$ accepted offer;
- accepted offer $\ne$ closing;
- closing $\ne$ recorded deed evidence; and
- named grantee $\ne$ ultimate economic ownership resolved.

## SHADOW index family

| Index | Question |
| --- | --- |
| TSI | How complete, current, verified, and transferable is title-and-rights state? |
| ASI | How much pressure is the asset under? |
| DPI | How much financing pressure is observable? |
| OOI | How incomplete or opaque is ultimate control/capital resolution? |
| CCI | Where is acquisition and ownership capital concentrating? |
| PVMI | Where did economic value move after disposition or restructuring? |
| PBOI | Does the asset warrant deeper People’s Trust feasibility review? |

A high PBOI means **review**, not **buy**. Formulas must be deterministic, versioned, evidence-linked, and accompanied by limitations. The v0.1 seed remains `NOT SCORED`.

## SHADOW CAMEL

SHADOW CAMEL is a proposed public-data condition methodology:

- C — Capital
- A — Asset Quality
- M* — public management-risk proxy
- E — Earnings
- L — Liquidity
- S* — publicly inferred sensitivity

The asterisks are mandatory. It is not an official regulatory CAMELS rating. The v0.1 workbook publishes headers only; scoring belongs in a later versioned-methodology PR using synthetic fixtures first.

## Orbitalys boundary

Orbitalys represents threat vectors and propagation paths and is accessed through a privately licensed M5 API integration. It can identify risks such as stale credentials, title-document tampering, beneficial-owner opacity, debt-data poisoning, jurisdiction bypass, or evidence suppression. It cannot determine legal authority or approve an action. The private service, endpoints, credentials, models, and proprietary outputs are not distributed or sublicensed by this repository. See the [adapter and license boundary](../reference/orbitalys-adapter/README.md).

## People’s Trust review

Nomination triggers evidence, title-state, debt/liens, rights/obligations, environmental, public-benefit, capital, community, and jurisdiction review. It creates no ownership, security, membership, investment, financing, or acquisition right. Every seed decision is `NEED MORE EVIDENCE`.
