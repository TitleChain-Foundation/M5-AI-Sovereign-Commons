# SHADOW M5Index

**Systemic Holdings, Assets, Debt & Ownership Watch**

> Old-world holdings. New-world deal flow.

SHADOW M5Index is the TitleChain Foundation public intelligence layer for tracking the movement and state of real-world assets, debt, ownership, rights, obligations and capital.

## Start here

- `registry/asset-index.csv` — compact current asset index
- `registry/assets.jsonl` — machine-readable full seed dataset
- `public/government/` — Government Buildings & Land view
- `public/cre/` — Commercial Real Estate view
- `public/farmland/` — Farmland view
- `public/assets/` — generated asset research pages
- `qr/` — permanent property QR codes
- `schemas/` — public ingestion/data contracts
- `reports/2026-Q3/` — Q3 2026 SHADOW CAMEL report release
- `methodology/` — public methodology

[TitleChain Foundation outreach routing](../docs/OUTREACH-ROUTING.md).

## Public contribution rule

**The public does not directly edit the canonical record. The public contributes evidence to the record.**

No GitHub issue, QR code, workbook row, report, public page, or nomination is
an acquisition claim. SHADOW CAMEL is not an official CAMELS rating, title
report, title insurance commitment, appraisal, legal advice, investment advice,
or claim that a listed asset is available for acquisition. A nomination creates no right.

Submission flow:

`SUBMITTED → SOURCE_VERIFIED → CORROBORATED → CANONICAL`

Also supported:

`DISPUTED · UNRESOLVED · REJECTED · CORRECTED · SUPERSEDED`

## Privacy

Public SHADOW stores public-safe evidence and entity/asset intelligence. Personal statements, account identifiers and other private records belong in private M5POD evidence, not the public Commons.

## People's Trust nomination boundary

SHD-029 and related People's Trust research are hold-state research leads. The
Q3 package may discuss a proposed $25 million tranche and possible title
transfer pathways. It does not establish title transfer, acquisition,
beneficial ownership, investment availability, or any right to control an
underlying property.
