Textual finding

The operative subsections (a)–(e) correspond in substance, including internal tax-code references and the three effective-date provisions. This table is a provision-by-provision legal comparison, not a machine-certified character-level diff. The principal structural change is Section 2 → Section 3001, nested inside a broad substitute; the survivor-only House amendment would restore the House short title and Section 2.

PAINE procedural note

The House can consider concurring in the Senate amendment with a further amendment substituting the survivor-only text. That is a proposed path, not an automatic right to a floor vote. If the House adopts it, the Senate must concur in the House amendment (or further differences must be resolved). Only identical text approved by both chambers can be presented to the President.
