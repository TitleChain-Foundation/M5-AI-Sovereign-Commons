H.R. 2347 public briefing

The House passed the Survivor Justice Tax Prevention Act on April 27, 2026. The Senate used the same bill number for the broader Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act and passed its amended version on November 19, 2026.

Where to read the survivor provision

Division C, Section 3001 of the Senate-engrossed text appears on pages 483–486. It proposes excluding certain nonpunitive damages received on account of sexual acts or sexual contact from federal gross income.

What Congress could do next

The Foundation’s recovered survivor-only proposal is a discussion draft for legislative counsel review. If the House adopts different text, both chambers would need to agree on identical text before presentment. GovInfo’s November 23, 2026 status update lists November 19 Senate passage as the latest action. It records no subsequent House action or enactment.
