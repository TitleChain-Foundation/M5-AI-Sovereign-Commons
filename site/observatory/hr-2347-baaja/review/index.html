Important nuance

The Senate proposal is not silent on AI: it includes a decentralized cross-network infrastructure mandate and congressional access to agency-specific AI materials. The identified gaps are additional explicit standards for agent authority, accountability, method certification, reproducible impact measurement, and post-permit outcome verification. Existing statutes may already impose relevant duties; a House amendment should clarify, not imply a blanket legal vacuum.
