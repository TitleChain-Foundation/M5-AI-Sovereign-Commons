Her authority · The three M5-Pens

Three pens. One human root.

The pens are signing contexts, not three separate keys. Her M5-CV, M5Bank account and M5Canon policies decide who can sign what.

I AMIdentity PenIdentity, credentials, roles, capacity and identity-related signatures.
I AUTHORIZEAgreement PenAgree, barter, trade, transact, transfer, pay and exchange value.
I CREATECreator PenOriginate, publish and license her works, designs, models and inventions.

Creator records preserve provenance and ownership claims; they do not by themselves establish legal intellectual-property rights.

iSWE · Women engineers
Proposed

Engineers who build the tools the Commons runs on.

iSWE activates women engineers from a personal M5POD to a co-op to a business. Discovery stays human-led and AI-assisted: she directs the work, agents help, and she signs what goes out.

01 · PersonalHer own M5PODHer research, credentials and private work, under her authority.
02 · Co-opA shared M5PODEngineers pool tools, data and discoveries with agreed terms.
03 · BusinessAn enterprise M5PODHer firm licenses what it builds and takes it to market.
The tools iSWE engineers create are published on the M5-OpenAPI Marketplace, where every iWBE business can use them.

Proposed initiative of the TitleChain Foundation. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Society of Women Engineers (SWE).

For women who build algorithms: her algorithm, her terms

  1. 01Create
  2. 02Preserve private evidence
  3. 03Register provenance
  4. 04Request review
  5. 05Choose license
  6. 06Publish or distribute
  7. 07Receive payment

She decides whether to publish open source, license commercially, keep the algorithm private or mix terms by release. The TitleChain record establishes attributable evidence and version history; M5Labs review establishes only the claims actually tested. Neither automatically grants copyright, patent rights or ownership.
