Open standards · Human possibility

A commons built around people.

AI agents can now act at machine speed across money, property, contracts and records. The M5 Sovereign Commons is the TitleChain Foundation’s public library and working laboratory for an economy where they act only on behalf of an accountable person, within limits anyone can check.

What is happening here.

  1. 01The rulesProposed open standards and machine-readable schemas for how people, businesses and their agents may act.

Creator Rights & Licensing

A featured M5Labs service for people developing original algorithms. The creator decides whether to publish open source, license commercially, keep the algorithm private, or use different terms for different releases.

  1. 01Create
  2. 02Preserve private evidence
  3. 03Register provenance
  4. 04Request review
  5. 05Choose license
  6. 06Publish or distribute
  7. 07Receive payment
TitleChain recordAttributable evidence and version history. Provenance attestation is proposed at $25 per version so individual inventors can afford it.
M5Labs technical reviewEstablishes only the specific mathematical or software claims actually tested.
What neither doesNeither automatically grants copyright, patent rights or ownership. A separately authorized Certificate of Title may address verified rights where legally appropriate.
