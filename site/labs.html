Beyond the frontier · AET

Aether: compute we cannot see

Compute will run in plasma and fields the eye cannot see, and some new mathematics already reaches past what we can imagine. Aether calculators work with the unknown under one rule: a human must understand a result before it can drive a decision.

Why Aether: it is the old name for the unseen medium once thought to fill space. It names what we cannot see but can still measure, and keeps the human the one who looks.

  1. 01RevealedA model or field surfaces a result. It is registered and held. Nothing acts on it yet.
  2. 02Interpreted by M5HUMA human principal works to explain what it shows, assisted by SI agents and formal checks such as Lean.
  3. 03UnderstoodExplained, reproduced and attested by a credentialed human. Only then can it enter a calculator.
Nothing unexplained acts on a person, a legal entity or the M5 economy.

  1. 01BuildWrite to the open calculator standard: inputs, outputs and a published method.
